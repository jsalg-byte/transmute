from Wikimedia Commons; no AI-generated image or CC BY-SA asset is bundled.

Each individual Commons file page was audited before import. The source work
was published in 1910; the pages identify Pamela Colman Smith as author and
mark the work public domain in the United States and in its country of origin.
The files are used as low-opacity backgrounds only, preserving the product’s
own visual language rather than reproducing a deck as the interface.

Future third-party additions require the same individual-file audit, direct
source URL, author, license, and attribution record. Only public-domain or CC0
files are eligible.
